Reproducer used during analysis:
  checked-in `python/examples/gluon/05-moe-bmm1-fused-gather.py`
  `ws_matmul_kernel`

Target:
  NVIDIA GB300

Issue:
  The warp-specialized matmul address lowering leaves two concrete register-only
  three-input OR trees in LLVM IR:
    %446 | (%380 | %381)
    %445 | (%412 | %413)
  PTXAS preserves each tree as two plain OR instructions instead of selecting a
  ternary logic instruction.

Before PTX:
  or.b32   %r239, %r238, %r213;
  or.b32   %r472, %r239, %r212;
  or.b32   %r241, %r240, %r233;
  or.b32   %r471, %r241, %r232;

After PTX:
  lop3.b32 %r472, %r238, %r213, %r212, 0xfe;
  lop3.b32 %r471, %r240, %r233, %r232, 0xfe;

Exactness:
  PTX `lop3.b32` with LUT `0xfe` computes `a | b | c` for every input bit, so
  it is bitwise identical to the two nested OR instructions for all 32-bit
  values.  The LLVM canonicalizer only uses this form for register-only trees;
  constants stay in normal ORs so they can still lower as immediates.

Effect:
  Hand-editing the emitted PTX first and assembling it with CUDA 13.1
  `ptxas-blackwell` reduced final GB300 SASS from 1240 to 1232 non-padding
  instructions for the checked-in `ws_matmul_kernel`.
