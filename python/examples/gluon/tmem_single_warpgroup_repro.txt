Reproducer used during analysis:
  a small Gluon TMEM load kernel with `num_warps=4`, plus the checked-in
  attention kernel in `python/examples/gluon/01-attention-forward.py`

Target:
  NVIDIA GB300

Issue:
  TMEM load/store lowering always masked `warpId & 3` before forming the row
  contribution to the TMEM address. In a CTA with only four warps, `warpId` is
  already in `[0, 3]`, so the mask is redundant.

Before PTX:
  and.b32 %r1470, %r1469, 6291456;

After PTX:
  the redundant mask is absent

Before SASS:
  ULOP3.LUT UR11, UR11, 0x600000, URZ, 0xc0, !UPT;

After SASS:
  the redundant `ULOP3.LUT` is absent

Effect:
  The checked-in attention kernel drops six TMEM address-mask instructions in
  the tested specialization while preserving the multi-warpgroup path for CTAs
  with more than four warps.
